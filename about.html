About Reef Solutions

Reef Solutions uses technology to solve business problems. With more than 25 years' experience, we work on process design, automation and business improvement. We start with the work that needs to be done, rather than a particular product or platform, and develop practical changes suited to each organisation's needs and constraints.

Experience Across Industries

Our clients range from small and medium-sized businesses to multinational organisations. Their operations, resources and requirements differ, so the challenges they face—and the right ways to address them—vary too. Experience working with organisations of different sizes helps us consider the context of each problem without losing sight of the practical outcomes sought.

Our Approach

Each engagement begins with understanding how work is currently done, what is causing difficulty and what needs to improve. We then consider options in the context of the organisation's processes, tools and resources. Recommendations are tailored to the situation and intended to be workable in day-to-day operations.

We also consider whether the expected benefits justify the costs, including the time and effort needed to make a change. If an option does not present a practical case, that should be clear before proceeding.

Technology with a Purpose

Technology is a means to address a business need, not an end in itself. Depending on the problem, improvement might involve redesigning a process, automating repeatable work or making better use of existing tools.

Where appropriate, Reef Solutions prefers open-source software and commonly available business tools. Their suitability depends on the requirements, support needs and total cost of each option. The aim is to choose tools that fit the work and deliver benefits that outweigh their costs.
